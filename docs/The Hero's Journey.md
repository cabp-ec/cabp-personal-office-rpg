## The Journey

### 1. Ordinary World

- Charlies' little office

### 2. Call to Adventure

Fulano watches the front of a house and its main door, then Charlie appears and the _"Introductions"_ dialogue starts. (Read [[Dialogues]] for details).

### 3. Refusal of the Call

Happens by default. If Fulano hesitates he can just ignore the candidate and wait or leave the website; he can come back any time.

### 4. Meeting the Mentor / Supernatural Aid

Happens by default. The game itself is the supernatural aid encouraging the hero to continue; simply because it's way more interactive and encouraging than a piece of paper.

### 5. Crossing the 1st Threshold

This happens when the player selects an option from the initial dialog. Then the _"Hello"_ dialogue starts. (Read [[Dialogues]] for details).

### 6. Tests, Allies, and Enemies

The office scene presents the tests: hidden information.

The only ally is Charlie, the candidate.

Enemies don't exist here, because _"we're family in this company"_...

The _"Welcome"_ dialogue starts. (Read [[Dialogues]] for details).

### 7. Approach to the Inmost Cave

This happens when the player finds the archive file cabinet, which is where the candidate stores all the history of his past projects and endeavors (dirty little secrets...?).

The _"Welcome"_ dialogue starts. (Read [[Dialogues]] for details).

### 8. The Ordeal

The ordeal for our hero is finding the relevant information for the search, which is hidden in the environment.

Let's remember that companies and recruiters usually prefer to read recent experience first. And this is not guaranteed in Charlie's little office, because the hero decides where to look.

> _Metric:_ We use this to accurately identify what Fulano de Tal read first and what he doesn't read at all.

### 9. Reward (a.k.a. Seizing the Sword)

The reward is to literally finding the candidate's information, may not happen in the order Fulano would have liked it but he found it, which is the important thing here.

1. Fulano clicks on an item, mini-tooltip shows dialog text _"What's this...?"_
2. Charlie walks toward the clicked item
3. If the item is interactive (i.e. have information _"hidden in it"_), Charlie kindly explains such information, otherwise he'll just say the name of the item e.g. _"That's a lamp..."_

### 10. The Road Back

This happens when Fulano is about to leave the office, he needs to find the standing desk.

### 11. The Resurrection (Climax)

Fulano watches the video presentation of the candidate and prints a copy of his résumé.

### 12. Return with the Elixir

Visitor leaves the office with all the material he needs:

1. A printed version of the résumé
2. Professional _"link-in-bio"_
3. Permalink to the candidate's profile
