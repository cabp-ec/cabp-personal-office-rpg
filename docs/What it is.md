## In short

**My Little Office** is an open-source, 2D pixel-art RPG platform for a more humane talent search and hiring process. Unlike traditional gamified assessments that force candidates to perform tasks, this platform gamifies the **_review process_** for the hiring manager or recruiter.

> My Little Office turns a candidate's CV into a 2D RPG experience.
> Recruiters don't read resumes.
> They play the candidate's story.
