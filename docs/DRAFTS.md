## Value Proposition

**Customer Jobs**

- **Finding Relevant Openings:** Searching digital boards for real, active career opportunities.
- **Submitting Applications:** Customizing resumes and cover letters for each specific role.
- **Navigating Portals:** Setting up accounts and filling out repetitive forms on various ATS platforms.
- **Interviewing:** Preparing for and completing multi-stage screening panels and skills assessments.
- **Securing an Income:** Obtaining a _stable_ job to pay for bills, rent, and basic living expenses.


**Gains**

- **Direct Human Interaction:** Receiving clear communication or a screening call from an actual hiring manager
	- In this digital era, we cannot guarantee human interaction
	- What we can do is
		- Making the interaction transparent: candidates know _exactly_ who's viewing his/her profile every time (activity logs) or has shown a more deep interest by signing the candidate's _guest book_
- **Process Transparency:** Knowing the clear timeline, salary range, and exact hiring steps from the start
	- Strict processes for recruiters and organizations: AUF rules!
- **Efficient Tracking:** Seeing exactly where an application stands in the employer pipeline without guesswork
	- When a process is reported as _"stuck"_, the AUF sends a plumber to check the pipeline in the complex, the candidate can see this on the screen
- [v] **Constructive Closure:** Receiving detailed, actionable feedback after an interview instead of generic automated rejections
- **Restored Purpose:** Rebuilding professional identity, structure, confidence, and social belonging
	- As an open-source initiative partnering with other initiatives and the overall community, we "by-default" help to restore professional identity
	- Our sponsor and community-vetted processes + the gamification approach help to rebuild structure, confidence and social belonging to all members

### Association of Unemployed Folks

Recruiters _must_ go to the AUF office to post a job. If unemployed folks wants to find a job, they just need to open the _"Jobs Board"_ from the control box.

**The AUF Building**

- Recruiters go to this building to _"register"_ before start posting job offers.
- The building has 12 stories, the lobby is where the recruiter/organization gets initially screened/filtered
- Stories 1 to 12 correspond to each sector of the GICS, each is where the recruiter/organization finish-up their registration

**The AUF Complex**

- This is where all recruiters and organizations looking for professionals _"work"_
- Recruiters are assigned to a _"space"_ inside the complex after their registration
- Each _"space"_ belongs to the organization, when another recruiter joins the platform he is automatically assigned to the same _"space"_
- Freelance recruiters are temporarily assigned to the _"space"_ of the organization they are hiring for; if they are hiring on behalf of several, they are temporarily assigned to several
- All _"spaces"_ grow in size automatically if more recruiters join the same _"space"_
- The whole complex is equipped with a pneumatic tube system
- Each _"space"_ es equipped with a  _dispatch station_, this is where recruiters finally _"post"_ a job offer by dropping capsule canister in it
