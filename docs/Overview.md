## What this is and what it's not

**Charlie's Little Office** is an open-source, 2D pixel-art RPG built using **Phaser 4, React, Elf, and TypeScript**. Unlike traditional gamified assessments that force candidates to perform tasks, this platform gamifies the **_review process_** for the hiring manager or recruiter.

The narrative architecture rejects sterile corporate structures, implementing Christopher Vogler's screenwriting framework, _**The Writer's Journey**_. Within this system:

- **The Recruiter is the Hero:** They are pulled from their "Ordinary World" of generic text-based candidate filtering into a "Special World" (a digital twin/tiled map representation of your physical home office).
- **The Candidate is the Mentor/Herald:** By interacting with objects in the office (e.g., clicking on a computer or a bookshelf), the player unlocks narrative milestones where the candidate provides contextual guidance, professional insight, and personal values.
