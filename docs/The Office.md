## Overview

This is the main scene, the environment in which the candidate's information is hidden.

---
## The SW Corner Desk

- The monitor hides the computer setup
	- Number of monitors
	- CPU Speed
	- Graphic card: yes/no
- The circular lamp indicates the media setup
	- Noise cancellation features: yes/no
	- Professional or semi-professional microphone: yes/no
	- Professional video camera: yes/no
- The totem indicates which figures (religious or not) the candidate likes
- The mesh metal tray indicates which projects or endeavors the candidate is currently working on or has recently working on (this is what recruiters want to read first)

---
## The Reading Nook

- The concert flag on the wall indicates the music the candidate likes
	- Charlie: _"That's one of my favorites, find the standing desk to talk about my fine musical taste."_
- The certificates on the wall indicate education and certification of the candidate (both formal and informal)
	- Charlie, after explaining the item: _"I'm sure you'll want to get a printed list of those before you leave. Use the printer bellow the standing desk to do so."_

---
## The SE Corner Desk

- The internet modem indicates the connection speed and link to a external speed test website
- The Lego figure indicates hobbies of the candidate

---
## The Archive File Cabinet

It contains all the details for every project and endeavor the candidate has engaged into in the past.

Dialog

1. Charlie: _"That's where I keep my dirty little secrets from the past hahaha."_
2. Fulano selects an option
	1. Forget it...
	2. Tell me about it

---
## The Beverage & Snack Decks

- The top deck is blocked
	- Charlie: _"That's secret, you need to reach the 'interview' level to find out..."_
- The middle deck is for beverages, indicates if the candidate likes to drink coffee or other beverages while working
- The lower deck if for snacks, indicates which snacks of food the candidate likes to eat while working

---
## The Book Shelves

- The NE shelf indicates which career-related books and readings the candidate likes or has read in the past
- The NW shelf indicates which not career-related books and readings the candidate likes or has read in the past (e.g. The Lord Of The Rings, Nostradamus, Cooking)

---
## The Standing Desk

- The open laptop computer allows Fulano to reproduce the video self-presentation of the candidate (from YouTube)
- The printer on the middle deck allows to download a copy of the candidate's résumé, the computer _"screen"_ shows up with options to select which parts to include in the printed version (e.g. formal education, previous experience, keywords)
